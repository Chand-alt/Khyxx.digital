Our story

Hi, this is Khye of KHYXX DIGITALS.

Honestly, KHYXX DIGITALS started because of our own wedding. I made invitations for our special day, then decided to post them online just for fun and inspiration. I never expected that someone would message me and ask if they could avail one too.

After posting more designs, one of them reached thousands of views, and little by little, people started using our templates for their weddings. Seeing our work become part of such meaningful moments still feels surreal to me.

For now, most of our designs are wedding-related, but hopefully, soon, we'll also create for other occasions and celebrations. Every template is made with love, excitement, and genuine happiness, because I know how special these moments are.

Thank you so much for supporting our small business. It truly means a lot to my husband and me.
